R.2 - Reasoning error: The agent has the right high-level frame but makes a localized reasoning mistake that derails the chain. R.4 - Task misunderstanding: The agent's mental model of the task is wrong at the structural level. Includes two related types: (1) Spec-level misunderstanding: misreading the question scope, confusing which attribute is being queried, misunderstanding role specifications, scope inflation (adding unrequested elements), and answering a subtly different question than what was asked. The chosen solution-space is over-restrictive, wrong-shape, or maps to a different problem than the one being solved. Includes "wrong algorithm class", "over-restrictive parameterization", and "false equivalence". Distinguishability test (R.4 vs R.2): could the candidate output, with arbitrarily many local bug fixes, ever satisfy the task? If no — the structural choice is wrong, it's R.4.

A.1 - Tool parameter/invocation error: Correct tool but wrong arguments, malformed call, missing required parameters, wrong configuration, or incomplete action sequence (omitting required precondition calls in stateful workflows).

C.2 - Communication failure: Withholding critical information from other agents, ignoring other agents' input or recommendations, failing to ask for clarification when instructions are ambiguous, and losing shared context across agent boundaries.
